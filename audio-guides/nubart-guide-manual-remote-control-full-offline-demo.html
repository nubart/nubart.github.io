Welcome Thank you for your interest in Nubart GUIDE. This page contains everything you need to try the demo we've prepared for you: a Nubart GUIDE running in full offline mode, with manual remote control layered on top so a guide can trigger tracks for the whole group in real time — built with your site's very limited connectivity in mind.
Why this setup Once a visitor's device has preloaded the guide, it keeps working with zero signal for the rest of the visit — that's what "full offline mode" covers below. Remote control is a separate mechanic layered on top: it needs only a very small amount of bandwidth to send each trigger, so it works even on a poor connection, but it does need some signal to reach the guide's device. Step 3 and the box below explain what to do on the rare occasion a spot on your site has genuinely zero connectivity.

How to get the best out of this demo

Visitor device Preload before you go dark. Scan the QR code and select a language while you still have connectivity. Wait for the guide to finish loading fully before heading into a low-signal area — that's the one moment in the whole visit where a connection is actually required.
Guide device Watch the track colour as you trigger. It tells you whether each track actually got through — see Step 3 below for what each colour means.
1

Set Up the Guide Device

On the Guide device, go to www.nub.art/customer/login and log in. If this is your first time, use the registration link in the box above to create your account first.

  1. Click Remote Control.
  2. You'll see the list of tracks you can trigger for the group.
The Guide device needs to stay connected for the whole tour to send trigger signals — but only a very small amount of bandwidth per trigger, so even a weak signal is normally enough.
Save the login link in your browser so you don't have to search for it next time. On a phone, add it to your home screen so it's always one tap away.
2

Set Up the Visitor Device and Preload the Guide

On the Visitor device, while you still have connectivity, scan the QR code at the top of this page (or tap the link below it).

  1. Select a language. This starts preloading the complete audio guide in that language onto the device.
  2. Wait for the preload to finish before moving into a low-signal area.
  3. No login, no app, no permissions required.
Optional, just for your own reassurance: once preloaded, switch to flight mode, close the browser or the tab, then rescan the QR code. The full guide should open immediately, without any connection.
3

Trigger Tracks Remotely

From the Guide device's Remote Control panel, click any track to send it, in real time, to every Visitor device with an open session. The track's colour tells you what's happening:

  • 🟢 Green — not triggered yet.
  • 🔴 Red, with a progress bar — sending now. Wait for it to turn grey before you trigger the next one.
  • 🔘 Grey — sent successfully. You can click it again any time to resend it.
  • 🟡 Yellow — couldn't send. Normally means zero connectivity at that spot.
If a track turns yellow, tell your participants its number and ask them to select it manually on their own device — since the guide is already preloaded, visitors can always browse and play any track themselves.
When the tour is over, click Restart Tour at the bottom of the Guide device's screen. It has no effect on the Visitor devices — it only resets all tracks back to green on your own screen, ready for the next group. Skip it, and every track will still show grey the next time you open Remote Control.

Triggered tracks play automatically on the visitor's phone by default. If you'd rather they just scroll to the top of the screen for a manual tap, let us know and we'll switch autoplay off.
