1. BACKGROUND

Nubart TRANSLATE is a real-time AI simultaneous interpretation service. Its standard data processing activities are designed so that event content is processed transiently, in accordance with the retention option selected by the Customer at the time of purchase.

Event data is the text that Nubart TRANSLATE produces while the event runs: the speech recognition output and its translations. Nubart processes it in real time and keeps or deletes it according to the retention option the Customer chose in the order form. A Post-Event Transcript is different. It is a document that Nubart creates after the event from the event data, at the Customer's request. Nubart places it in the Customer's Assets, where it stays until the Customer deletes it. The Terms and Conditions and the DPA cover event data. This Authorization covers the creation and delivery of the Post-Event Transcript.

A Post-Event Transcript is not a standard deliverable of Nubart TRANSLATE and is not included in the scope of the Customer's existing DPA. Where a Customer explicitly requests one, Nubart may accommodate this on a strictly exceptional, case-by-case basis, subject to execution of this Authorization.

This Authorization is a documented instruction from the Customer to Nubart under applicable data protection law, authorizing the specific additional processing activities described herein.

3. CUSTOMER ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

By signing this Authorization, the Customer confirms, as data controller, each of the following:

  1. Explicit request and legal basis. The Customer explicitly requests that Nubart create a Post-Event Transcript for the event identified in Section 2. The Customer confirms that this request is a documented instruction and that it has a valid legal basis for this processing under the data protection law that applies to it.
  2. Extended retention. The Customer acknowledges that creating a Post-Event Transcript requires Nubart to make working copies of event data and to keep them temporarily, beyond the transient processing model of the standard service configuration. The Customer authorizes Nubart to keep these copies for as long as is necessary to create and deliver the Post-Event Transcript, and instructs Nubart to delete them within 30 calendar days of the delivery date of the Post-Event Transcript. The event data itself stays subject to the retention option the Customer chose in the order form.
  3. Additional processing activities. The Customer acknowledges that creating a Post-Event Transcript involves processing activities not covered by the standard DPA, including deduplication, reformatting, and AI-assisted post-processing of raw event output. The Customer authorizes these additional activities solely for the purpose of creating the Post-Event Transcript requested herein.
  4. Additional processing tools. The Customer acknowledges that creating a Post-Event Transcript may require the use of additional processing tools or sub-processors beyond those used for the standard Nubart TRANSLATE service configuration. Nubart will ensure that such processing is subject to appropriate contractual and technical safeguards consistent with applicable data protection law.
  5. Quality and nature of output. The Customer acknowledges that Nubart TRANSLATE is a live simultaneous interpretation system. Its output reflects the nature of real-time AI processing and does not constitute professional transcription. The Post-Event Transcript is delivered as generated, without certified editorial review. It does not constitute official minutes or a certified record of the event.
  6. Test session content. The Customer acknowledges that raw event data may contain content from technical test sessions conducted before or after the event. Nubart will make reasonable efforts to exclude identifiable test content.
  7. Liability. Nubart's liability in connection with the Post-Event Transcript is governed by Section 9 of the Terms and Conditions. The Customer is responsible for the purposes for which it uses the Post-Event Transcript.
  8. Authority to give instructions. The Customer is duly authorized to give instructions to Nubart within the framework of this Authorization.
  9. No precedent. The Customer acknowledges that Nubart's acceptance of this Authorization does not create any obligation or expectation that a Post-Event Transcript will be provided for future events, and does not modify the standard scope of the Nubart TRANSLATE service or DPA for any other purpose.

4. DELIVERY AND DELETION

Upon completion, Nubart will make the Post-Event Transcript available in the Assets section of the Customer's password-protected Customer Area. The Customer logs in to download it. Nubart does not send Post-Event Transcripts by email. The Post-Event Transcript will be provided in a commonly used document format (e.g., PDF or DOCX). No specific format, structure, or length is guaranteed.

Following delivery, Nubart will delete all intermediate processing artefacts within 30 calendar days of the delivery date, in addition to any obligations under the Customer's original DPA retention selection. The Post-Event Transcript itself stays in the Assets section until the Customer deletes it.
