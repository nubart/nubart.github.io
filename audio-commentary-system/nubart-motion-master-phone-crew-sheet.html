Master phone: crew instructions

This phone starts the commentary on the passengers' phones. If it is off, locked or closed, the commentary stops.

Always

  • Place the phone at the highest point, near a window, away from radars.
  • Keep it plugged in.
  • Keep the screen unlocked.
  • Keep the “Remote Control” tab open for the whole journey.

Reconnect after a restart, or if the page was closed

  1. Unlock the phone.
  2. Open Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). Do not use other browsers.
  3. Go to www.nub.art/customer/login and log in:
  4. Select “Remote Control”.
  5. Select the right vessel.
  6. If the phone asks for location access, tap “Allow”.

Maintenance: restart the phone every two to three months, then reconnect as above.

The Remote Control screen

Each track changes colour:

  • Green: not started yet.
  • Red, with a progress bar: being sent now.
  • Grey: sent.
  • Yellow: could not be sent, normally because there is no connectivity at that spot.

The icon on each track shows how it starts: a pin starts it at a location, a clock after a set time, and a hand when you tap it. If all tracks start the same way, no pin or clock is shown. After tapping a track, wait until it turns grey before starting another one.

“Restart Tour” at the bottom is optional. It only turns the tracks back to green on this screen, ready for the next tour.

Troubleshooting

A passenger says a track did not start?
Ask them to press play on that track in the guide.

No tracks are starting?
Check that this phone is plugged in, unlocked and showing the Remote Control for the right vessel. If not, reconnect (see front).

A track starts too early, too late or not at all?
Nubart can adjust it remotely. Email the name of the trigger and the vessel to info@nubart.eu.
