Fine-Tuning Your Audio Guide

Temporary Master smartphone — step-by-step instructions

Temporary Master smartphone For your test routes, please use one smartphone as the temporary Master smartphone. It sends the boat's real GPS position to us while you sail your normal route, so our team can fine-tune the audio guide remotely. It's the only phone you need for this — nothing to install for passengers.

Why we're asking for this

  • We need real route data to fine-tune exactly when and how long each track plays.
  • We can do this remotely. Because the signal reaches us as you sail, our team can fine-tune from our office — no repeated visits needed.
  • It's only for the test routes. Once testing is complete, we'll let you know what happens next.

Before You Set Off

  • Use the one phone set up as your Master smartphone (see above).
  • Use Chrome if you are using an Android. iPhone with Safari also works, though it may need a couple of extra permission steps — see Troubleshooting.
  • Make sure Location Services are switched on and allow your browser to use your precise location.
  • Keep the phone connected to power for the entire route.
  • Place it at the highest point of the vessel, near a window.
Please don't use Firefox nor other browsers for this. It doesn't have full access to location data and can cause tracks to trigger at the wrong time.
2

Log In and Open Remote Control

Open the login link above (use the registration link first if this is your first time).

Once logged in:

  1. Tap Remote Control in the menu.
  2. Select your tour from the list.
  3. Keep the Remote Control screen open and the phone unlocked for the entire route.
If the screen locks or the browser closes, triggering will stop.
3

Run Your Route as Normal

That's it — just sail your usual route with the phone in place. Tracks will trigger automatically as you pass each point. This allows our team to test and fine-tune the guide before it goes live for passengers.

You don't need to operate the phone during the route. No need to note anything down; our team will be following along.

If a track plays too early, too late, or not at the expected location, please don't try to fix anything yourself — our team will investigate this remotely.
Troubleshooting
Nothing is triggering at all

Location permissions can exist at three levels — check all of them:

  1. System level: your phone's location services are switched on
  2. Browser level: your browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone) has location access
  3. Domain level: nub.art is allowed to use your location when prompted

Also make sure you're using Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone) — not Firefox nor any other browser. Try holding the phone closer to a window or moving it to a higher position.

GPS works on Chrome but not on Safari (iPhone)

Safari on iPhone requires geolocation enabled at three levels:

  1. System: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → ON
  2. Browser: same menu → find Safari → set to While Using the App
  3. Domain: Settings → Apps → Safari → Settings for Websites → Location → not Deny. Or tap AA in Safari's address bar → Website Settings → Location → Allow.

Also confirm Precise Location is enabled, then reload the page.

Remote Control screen disappeared / session expired

Log back in at www.nub.art/customer/login, go to Remote Control, and select your tour again. To avoid this, keep the screen on and the browser open at all times.
