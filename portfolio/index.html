Nubart GUIDE customers worldwide

229 projects · 27 countries · 5 continents

Nubart GUIDE is a QR-code audio guide that runs in the visitor's browser, with no app to download. Museums, castles, cathedrals, show caves, stadiums, university campuses, city tours and sightseeing boats in 27 countries use it for self-guided tours. Examples include the Albertina in Vienna, the Alcázar of Segovia, the FC Barcelona Museum and the Ohio State Reformatory. On boats and buses, the audio can start automatically by GPS with Nubart MOTION. More than 70% of these customers also asked Nubart to produce their audio guide content.
