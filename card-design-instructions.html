Design instructions for cards

These instructions apply to printed cards for both Nubart GUIDE and Nubart LIVE. The technical specifications, code generation, and production process are identical for both products.

Card design

Our cards should have two blank fields: one for the QR code and one for the alphanumeric code. You will receive the cards with the codes printed in these fields.

Below are the technical requirements that your designer should be aware of. We cannot accept designs that do not meet these parameters. Please use a large, legible font, especially for the instructions on the back.

Our cards are printed on environmentally friendly materials (high-density solid board, dense enough to feel like plastic though it's 100% paper fiber, or bio-PVC or recycled PVC). Please pick the one that corresponds to the material you have chosen for your cards.

At Nubart, we offset all CO₂ emissions generated during production, including those from transporting raw materials, the entire printing process, and packaging and shipping to the final destination. To emphasize this, we recommend adding the following icon:

Download all the icons

Technical requirements

  • Format: PDF/X-3 with embedded fonts
  • Two-sided, four-color (CMYK) offset printing
  • Images in high resolution (max. 300 dpi)
  • Safe area: 81.7 x 50 mm
  • Finished size: 85.7 x 54 mm
  • Bleed area: 89 x 58 mm

Blank areas

  • 25 x 25 mm (for the QR code)
  • 30 x 5 mm (for the alphanumeric code)
